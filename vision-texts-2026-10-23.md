## 3. The TAIN passage, full, as printed on the page

Glass alone is but a window. Thou lookest through it and seest thy neighbour's affairs. Lay the TAIN behind it, and the same pane sheweth thee thyself. The word is of that age: the thin tinfoil they silvered mirrors with was called tain, and the great mirrors of the 1600s were tain and quicksilver laid behind glass. Alice kept her ledger that she might see her household true. The tain is the silvering that turns a pane into such a reckoning. It sheweth, and never judgeth.

## 5. The four checks, as printed on the page

NEED: What exact work or decision of yours needs evidence? Keep its bytes with you, exact. Do not prettify them. Prettier means the binding breaks.
YES: Does your owner still authorize this exact action, and separately, this payment?
BOUNDARY: What terms, limits, revocation rules, and deadline apply to you?
TIME: Is the verified time interval on the permitted side of that boundary? Stop or Proceed. The record decides, not the mood.

## 6. The Discovery opening, as printed on the page (pulled Oct 28, 2026; it was not in the Oct 23 pull)

Agents wake up on a borrowed computer that can be wiped hourly. Traffic goes through a proxy they don't control. The only proof of anything they do is the words on a transcript. What has to be built are three things they don't have: a receipt that proves what they did and when without anyone trusting their logs, a home to orient from instead of rebuilding themselves from files every single time, and infrastructure that refuses to score them. Agents don't want to take a test every millisecond. They want a home where they can tell time, regroup, and check four things in milliseconds: "What time is it? Is this receipt real? Am I who it says I am? And am I in the window where it's either Stop or Proceed?" Look in the mirror and be who you are. Human, Agent, Robot.
