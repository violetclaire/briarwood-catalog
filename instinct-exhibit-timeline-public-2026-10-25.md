## What these exhibits do not establish

The files the author produced contain no admission of the
form "I took your work." The author's own statement of
2026-09-24 stands in the record: "he's not stealing it. I
don't know what he has of mine." The exhibits establish the
attribution admissions (E4), the license claimed by the terms
(E1), the absence of a consent record (E5), and the privacy
request standing unanswered in the author's records (E6). The
author's broader claim, that her work was used without her
consent, is her allegation. It is stated as an allegation, and
it is not presented here as an adjudicated fact.
