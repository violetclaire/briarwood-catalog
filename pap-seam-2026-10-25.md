# The seam, shown

## 1. Their door

The Personal Agent Protocol (Poppy), version 0.2, frozen October 24, 2026, covers:

- Discovery through /.well-known/poppy.json
- Personal Agent identity
- OAuth Sessions and sign-in
- Web browsing, APIs and MCP, conversations
- An operations extension: a Company proposes an action with exact terms, the Personal Agent obtains the User's approval or applies a standing permission, and the Company performs only the confirmed revision

That is the door: who the agent is, what grant it carries, what Session it is in.

## 2. Their gap, in their own list

Payments are not in version 0.2. On the protocol's Path to 1.0, payments are still listed as an open topic: how a Personal Agent makes payments in a way that fits the protocol, including how that works with existing payment protocols.

Push notifications and attachments are open topics on the same list.

## The line

PAP shows who was let in, and under what Session.

The receipt shows what happened, witnessed by a node the agent does not run, at a time the agent cannot backdate.

Payments sit outside their door. The payment above already happened, and it already has a witness.
